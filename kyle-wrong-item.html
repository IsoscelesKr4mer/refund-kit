The box that arrived as a Simpsons DVD

You paid $806.09 for a sealed 2026 Topps Mint Disney NYCC box. What turned up in Kent on 19 October was a torn Priority Mail box with a Simpsons Blu-ray in it, carrying two separate Post Office stamps saying it arrived open. You do not need to prove who took it. You need to file the right request, with the right reason, before 18 November.

The Post Office documented this for you before you noticed. They stamped the label RECEIVED UNSEALED in red and taped the box RECEIVED IN DAMAGED CONDITION. That is a carrier admission, in writing, on the parcel itself. Most people in your position have nothing but their own word.

Because that Venmo payment went through eBay's own checkout rather than directly to the seller, the Money Back Guarantee applies to it in full. A Venmo payment sent outside eBay would have left you with nothing. This one does not.

The thing that makes this look hopeless, and is not

The listing says Returns not accepted, and because of that your order screen shows no return button. That is almost certainly why this feels stuck.

"Returns not accepted" only blocks a change of mind. It has no effect on a wrong item. eBay's Money Back Guarantee covers an item that does not match the listing regardless of the seller's return policy, and a Blu-ray in place of a sealed card box is as clear a mismatch as that rule ever sees. If the button is missing, the route is Help & Contact, not giving up.

Step one · open the request today

1

Pick "doesn't match the description". Nothing else.

The reason you choose decides whether this is won or argued about, so this is the single most important click on the page.

ReasonWhat it does
Doesn't match description / received the wrong itemCorrect. Covers you even on a no-returns listing. Seller pays return shipping if eBay asks for the DVD back.
Arrived damagedWrong. It concedes the right item arrived and broke, which is not what happened and is a weaker claim.
Didn't arriveWrong. Tracking shows delivered, so this gets closed automatically and you have burned a request.

Where to find it. My eBay, then Purchases, then the order, then "Return this item". If that option is not there because returns are off, go to Help & Contact, then Returns and refunds, then tell it you received the wrong item and start the request from there. If the app still will not offer it, open a chat with an agent and say the sentence in step two. An agent can open it manually.

If eBay filed it as "damaged" anyway, you are still fine. Their assistant routes a lot of these into the damaged bucket whatever you type, and that happened here. Both reasons sit under the same Money Back Guarantee, so the refund and the seller-paid return are unchanged.

The one thing worth doing: add a message to the open case saying plainly that the item was not in the package and a different item, a Simpsons Blu-ray, was inside, and attach the photos again. A "damaged" claim is the one an uncooperative seller can try to push toward a USPS claim instead of a refund, and that sentence in the record closes the door on it. Do not close the case and open a new one.

Your deadline is 18 November. Thirty days from the 19 October delivery. That is plenty of time and it is also the reason not to spend three weeks negotiating with the seller first. Open the request, then talk.

Step two · what to write in it

2

Facts, in order, with nothing added

Keep it to what is on the parcel and in the order. Every sentence here is something eBay can confirm without taking your word for it.

Request text
I ordered a sealed 2026 Topps Mint Disney NYCC Box (Thursday), order 22-15273-30464, $806.09 total.

The package was delivered on 19 October. It did not contain the item. Inside was a Simpsons Blu-ray.

The box arrived open and USPS documented it. The shipping label is stamped "RECEIVED UNSEALED" in red, and the box is taped with USPS "RECEIVED IN DAMAGED CONDITION" tape. The box is torn along one seam. I have photographs of the label, the tape, the torn box and the Blu-ray, and I have kept all of the packaging.

I am requesting a full refund of $806.09. I have not opened or used anything and I can return the Blu-ray and the packaging if you need it.

Attach the photos you already took. The label with the red stamp, the outside of the box with the USPS tape, the torn seam, and the Blu-ray sitting in the box. Four images is enough. They are better evidence than any paragraph.

Step three · then message the seller

3

Friendly, because he is probably not the problem

harrism84 is at 100% over 135 feedback with 150 sales and a normal mixed collectibles inventory, and the return address fits somebody who was actually at the show. That is not the shape of a scammer. He may well be finding out from your message that one of his parcels was opened.

He also has a reason to help: Priority Mail Flat Rate only carries $100 of insurance by default, so on a $720 box he is exposed too and will want the USPS claim to go through. Cooperation is in his interest, and a civil message is what gets it.

Message to seller
Hi, I wanted to let you know directly rather than just file and go quiet.

The Mint Disney box arrived today and the package had been opened in transit. USPS stamped the label "RECEIVED UNSEALED" and taped the box with their "received in damaged condition" tape. Inside there was a Simpsons Blu-ray instead of the box.

I am not accusing you of anything. I have opened a request with eBay because of the time limit, not because I think you did this.

Two things that would help: a photo of the item as you packed it, if you took one, and whether you insured the parcel above the $100 Priority Mail covers. I have kept the box, the tape, the label and the Blu-ray, so you have everything you need for a USPS claim. Happy to send it all back or to the Post Office if that helps you.

Do not let him talk you into closing the request. A seller who offers to refund you directly if you close the case is asking you to give up the only protection you have, whether or not he means well. Keep the case open until the money is actually back.

Step four · report the mail theft

4

Free, five minutes, and it is already half done

File at www.uspis.gov/report, choosing Mail Theft, with the tracking number 9505506593916287317227. The Postal Inspection Service already has the unsealed stamp on their own record, so you are confirming something they logged rather than making a claim they have to assess.

This does not get your money back. eBay does that. It matters because a $720 parcel opened between Pearl River and Kent is worth somebody looking at, and because it strengthens the seller's insurance claim, which is what makes him easy to deal with.

Three arguments not to make

Each of these feels like it helps and each one hands eBay a reason to read the rest of you more carefully.

Do not state that it was stolen in transit. You do not know that, and you do not need it. The claim that wins is "the item was not in the box". The moment you name a culprit you have made an assertion somebody can disprove, and you have invited eBay to treat this as a carrier problem rather than a refund.

Do not lead with the 7.20 oz on the label. It is tempting, because a weight feels like proof. It is not. A sealed Mint Disney tin and a Blu-ray case weigh about the same, so that number is consistent with either story and settles nothing. Put it in front of eBay as a gotcha and the one piece of maths in your report is the piece that is wrong.

Do not accuse the seller of swapping it. Separate from whether it is true, eBay does not require a culprit to refund you, and an accusation converts a clean not-as-described claim into a dispute with two sides. It also costs you the one person who can file the USPS claim and produce a packing photo.

What not to do with the parcel

  • Do not throw any of it awayThe box, the USPS tape, the label with the red stamp, and the Blu-ray. eBay can require you to return the wrong item before refunding, and the seller pays that shipping. Binning it is the one way to lose a case you have already won.
  • Do not take payment outside eBayVenmo direct, Zelle, Cash App, none of it is covered. Your protection exists because the money moved through eBay's checkout. Keep it there.
  • Do not leave feedback yetIt changes nothing about the refund and it removes your reason to stay civil. Decide after the money is back.

Before you file

What this should end as. A full $806.09 back, probably without an argument, because the Post Office wrote your evidence on the outside of the parcel and the seller has his own reason to want this resolved. The only way it goes wrong is running out the clock or throwing out the box.
